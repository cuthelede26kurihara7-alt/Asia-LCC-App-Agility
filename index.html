Measuring Business Agility Through Digital Behavior

How Airlines Adapt Their Digital Platforms in Asia’s Competitive Aviation Market

Why This Matters

The airline industry has always been shaped by competition.

Historically, airlines competed through aircraft fleets, international routes, and pricing strategies.

Today, competition is increasingly happening through digital experiences.

Mobile apps are no longer only booking tools. They have become platforms where airlines interact with customers, manage services, and continuously improve the travel experience.

But one question remains:

How can we measure whether a company is truly agile in a digital economy?

Research Question

Can digital behavior reveal business agility?

Business agility is often discussed as the ability to respond quickly to change. However, agility is difficult to measure.

This project uses airline app updates as a visible indicator of how companies adapt.

  • How often airlines improve their digital platforms
  • What types of improvements they prioritize
  • How digital strategies differ between low-cost and full-service carriers

Data

I analyzed mobile app release notes from four airlines connected to the Asian aviation market.

Key Insights

Digital agility does not have one definition.

The analysis shows that companies adapt according to their business models.

  • AirAsia: Scaling customer experience through digital platforms.
  • Scoot: Balanced digital experimentation across multiple areas.
  • ZIPAIR: Building digital infrastructure to support growth.
  • JAL: Maintaining reliability and operational stability.

Conclusion

Digital transformation is often measured through technology adoption.

This project explores another perspective:

Digital behavior can reveal business agility.

By observing what companies improve, how frequently they improve, and where they invest their effort, we can better understand how organizations adapt in competitive markets.
