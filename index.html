Measuring Business Agility Through Digital Behavior

How Airlines Adapt Their Digital Platforms in Asia’s Competitive Aviation Market

Introduction

In today's digital economy, business agility is increasingly reflected in how companies continuously improve their digital products and services.

Airline mobile apps provide a unique window into this behavior. Beyond booking flights, airline platforms now connect customers with loyalty programs, payments, travel services, and operational communication.

This project explores how airline app update patterns reveal different approaches to digital agility.

Research Question

How can we measure business agility through digital behavior?

This project measures digital agility through two dimensions:

  • Speed: How frequently airlines improve their digital platforms.
  • Strategic Focus: What types of improvements airlines prioritize.

Key Findings

Digital agility is not one-size-fits-all. Airlines adapt differently depending on their business models and strategic priorities.

  • AirAsia: Customer-experience-focused digital strategy.
  • Scoot: Balanced improvements across multiple digital areas.
  • ZIPAIR: Growth-oriented strategy combining operational improvement and network expansion.
  • JAL: Reliability and maintenance-focused strategy as a full-service carrier.

Methodology

App release histories were collected from Apple App Store version histories for AirAsia, Scoot, ZIPAIR, and Japan Airlines (JAL).

Each release note was manually classified into four categories: Customer Experience, Feature Development, Maintenance, and Network Expansion.

Limitations

Release notes represent publicly communicated improvements and may not capture all internal development activities. However, they provide a transparent view into how companies communicate and prioritize digital evolution.
