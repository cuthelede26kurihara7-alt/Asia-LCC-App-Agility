# Measuring Business Agility Through Digital Behavior

## How Airlines Adapt Their Digital Platforms in Asia’s Competitive Aviation Market

![Project Banner](https://via.placeholder.com/1200x400)

---

## Project Overview

In today's digital economy, business agility is increasingly reflected in how companies continuously improve their digital products and services.

This project explores how airline mobile app update patterns can reveal different approaches to digital agility.

By analyzing release notes from four airlines with strong Asian market connections — AirAsia, Scoot, ZIPAIR, and Japan Airlines (JAL) — this project examines:

- How frequently airlines update their digital platforms
- What types of improvements they prioritize
- How different business models shape digital strategies

---

## Research Question

**How can we measure business agility through digital behavior?**

This project defines digital agility through two dimensions:

1. **Speed**
   
   How frequently companies improve their digital platforms.

2. **Strategic Focus**

   Where companies allocate their digital improvement efforts.

---

# Key Findings

## 1. Digital agility starts with continuous improvement

Airline apps are not static products. Companies continuously release updates to improve customer experience, add features, maintain reliability, and support business growth.

## 2. Different airlines demonstrate different forms of agility

The analysis reveals different digital strategies:

| Airline | Digital Focus |
| --- | --- |
| AirAsia | Customer Experience focused |
| Scoot | Balanced improvements across features and experience |
| ZIPAIR | Growth and operational expansion |
| JAL | Reliability and maintenance focused |

## 3. Business models influence digital priorities

Low-cost carriers often use digital platforms to scale customer interactions efficiently, while full-service carriers emphasize reliability and service quality.

# Methodology

## Data Source

Release notes were collected from Apple App Store version histories for:

- AirAsia
- Scoot
- ZIPAIR
- Japan Airlines (JAL)

## Data Classification

Each release note was manually classified into one of four categories:

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Customer Experience | Improvements to user journey and usability |
| Feature Development | New functions and capabilities |
| Maintenance | Bug fixes, stability, and reliability improvements |
| Network Expansion | Changes supporting airline growth and expansion |

---

# Limitations

Release notes represent publicly communicated improvements and may not capture all internal development activities.

However, they provide a transparent view into how companies communicate and prioritize digital evolution.
